About Kriya

Built by a business owner, for business owners.

I built Kriya for my own business after years of enquiries living in WhatsApp, a diary and Excel, and a stint on a well-known CRM that was good software built for somebody else. My team runs on it every day.

Now I set it up for other businesses that sell the same way: through enquiries, quotations and follow-ups. You deal with me directly, not a helpdesk.

Is this for you?

Built for one kind of business. Maybe yours.

It's for you if · tap what's true
  • You run a business of roughly 1 to 50 people
  • You sell through enquiries, quotations and follow-ups, not a checkout page
  • Enquiries come in on WhatsApp, calls, walk-ins, IndiaMART or referrals
  • You're a distributor, manufacturer, trader, dealer, interiors or furnishing firm, AV or industrial supplier

It's probably not for you if
  • You get fewer than 10 enquiries a month
  • Your team already uses a CRM properly, every day
  • You sell online at a fixed price, with no quotations

If that's you, I'll say so in our first chat and save us both the time.

Before you decide

The part most brochures leave out.

What is already true
  • Kriya runs a real business every working day. It was built for one, not assembled for a brochure.
  • Your data stays yours. It is never shared or used for anyone else.
  • You deal directly with the person who built it, not a helpdesk.
  • Export everything, any time, without asking anyone.
What is not there yet
  • No app-store app. It runs in your phone's browser, and works well there.
  • iPhone contact import isn't built yet. Android works.
  • Detailed permission levels are set up on request.
  • It is young software. If something breaks, I fix it.
