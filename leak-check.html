The enquiry leak check

You already have a system. It's just not software.

It lives across a WhatsApp inbox, a diary, three Excel files and one person's memory. It works, right up until the day it doesn't. First, see where it leaks. Then work out what it costs.

The enquiry you can't find

Serious budget, good conversation, back in March. You remember the chat. You don't remember the number, and scrolling WhatsApp for a half-remembered name is not a system.

02

The follow-up nobody made

Nobody forgot on purpose. Nothing reminded anyone. The quotation went out, the customer went quiet, and the file closed itself without a decision ever being made.

The quotation that ate the evening

Open last month's Excel, swap the items, fix the totals, check the discount maths twice, export a PDF that still looks like a spreadsheet.

The business that lives in one head

One person knows every customer, every price, every hesitation. The day they take leave, the whole business slows to their reply speed.

05

The customer who simply went quiet

Bought elsewhere? Decided against it? Needed one more nudge on a Tuesday? Without the answer, nothing improves next quarter.
