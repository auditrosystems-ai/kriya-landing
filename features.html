Features

Four things that stop the leak.

Capture every enquiry, follow up on time, send a quotation that wins the room, and talk to customers on WhatsApp. Everything below is in the product today. Tap, drag and press play: it all works.

01 · Capture

Nothing gets lost on the way in

Speak, type, or paste a screenshot of a WhatsApp chat, a business card or an enquiry form. Kriya fills in the contact: name, number, requirement, urgency. Inside a customer's record the same words become a note, never a duplicate.

"Met Sharma ji, architect, needs a quote for 3 sites, follow up Friday."
✓ Contact: Sharma jiArchitectNeeds: quote, 3 sites🔥 Warm lead⏰ Task: follow up Friday
02 · Follow up

Nobody has to remember

"Follow up in two days" becomes a dated task on the right person's screen. Every deal sits in a pipeline built around your own stages, and you can ask plain questions instead of building filters.

"Which quotations are still pending?" "Hot leads not contacted this week."
03 · Quote

Quotations that win the room

Tick items from your catalogue and Kriya builds a multi-page proposal on your letterhead: cover, one clear investment figure, itemised list with photos, specifications, terms and team. Fix a product detail once and every future quote uses the better version.

Message a customer from their record using your own number, in the chat they already know. The conversation stays attached to the customer. Pro
