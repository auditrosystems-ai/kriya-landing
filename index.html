What Kriya does

Four things that keep every deal moving

Simple enough for anyone on your team. Works on your phone and laptop, in the browser.

Basic & Pro

Every lead in one place, with reminders

Add a lead in seconds, from any channel. Set a follow-up date and Kriya reminds the right person on the right day.

  • Leads, customers, dealers, architects: your own categories
  • Dated follow-up tasks for each person
  • See today's calls the moment you open it
Basic & Pro

A branded quotation in under a minute

Tick products from your catalogue. Kriya builds a clean, multi-page quotation on your letterhead, with photos, GST and discounts worked out.

  • Your logo, colours and terms
  • Product photos and links
  • Saved to the customer, ready to resend
